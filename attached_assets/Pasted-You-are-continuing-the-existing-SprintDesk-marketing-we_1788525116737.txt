==================================================
01. PRODUCT UNDERSTANDING
==================================================

SprintDesk is a connected workspace for:

- Personal task management
- Team task management
- Sprint execution
- Team visibility
- Workflow automation

The most important product differentiation is:

Most task and project management tools focus primarily on team collaboration.

Most personal productivity tools focus primarily on individual work.

SprintDesk connects both contexts.

A user can:

1. Capture a thought or task instantly.
2. Leave it unorganized initially.
3. Triage it later.
4. Decide whether it belongs to:
   - Personal work
   - Team work
5. Move it into the correct workspace.
6. Track progress.
7. Give managers visibility.
8. Automate repetitive actions.

The central product philosophy is:

Capture personally.
Organize intelligently.
Execute together.

The core transformation is:

SCATTERED THOUGHT
        ↓
CAPTURE
        ↓
TRIAGE
        ↓
PERSONAL TASK FLOW
        OR
TEAM SPRINT BOARD
        ↓
TRACK PROGRESS
        ↓
AUTOMATE

The homepage must communicate this transformation visually and emotionally.

Do not reduce SprintDesk to:

"Kanban + Calendar + Automation"

Those are features.

The STORY is:

A thought becomes organized work.
Organized work becomes focused execution.
Focused execution becomes visible team progress.
